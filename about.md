# About

I'm **Velkris**, a cybersecurity practitioner and red team operator. My focus is
offensive security and the defensive tradecraft around it - malware triage,
detection engineering, and understanding exactly where and why a payload gets
caught.

I build open-source tooling for that work: [OffsetInspect](/projects/), a
PowerShell toolkit for detection-boundary and static analysis, and
[OffsetScan](/projects/), its native Rust companion for corpus-scale triage.
Both are read-only by design and never disable or reconfigure endpoint
protection.

This site is where I keep original write-ups on how these techniques and
mechanisms actually work - grounded in public research, in my own words.

Find me on [GitHub](https://github.com/warpedatom) and
[X (@warped_atom)](https://x.com/warped_atom).
