When I put up the first post here, it was a placeholder and a promise —
*"I'll be sharing notes from my pentesting labs, red team tools, and
certification journey."* This is the follow-through.

The site got a full rebuild. New look, and more importantly, real content:

- **Write-ups** on how the mechanisms actually work — [Kerberos end to end](/),
  golden/silver/diamond ticket forgery, delegation abuse, domain dominance,
  AMSI internals, imphash, entropy, and TLSH clustering. All in plain terms,
  from public research, with a defender's angle attached.
- A [**Projects**](/projects/) page for the tooling I maintain —
  **OffsetInspect** (PowerShell detection-boundary + static triage) and
  **OffsetScan** (its native Rust companion for corpus-scale work).

A note on scope, because it matters: I keep detailed notes from paid courses,
and those stay private — publishing course labs isn't fair to the people who
build them. What lands here is my own writing on publicly documented topics,
and my own code. If a technique is in MITRE ATT&CK or a vendor's research, it's
fair game to explain well; if it's someone's lab solution, it isn't.

What's next: more of the same — short, practical explainers that connect an
attack technique to what a defender sees, plus deep-dives on the tooling as it
grows. If there's something you want unpacked, the topic tracker is open in
[Issues](https://github.com/warpedatom/warpedatom.github.io/issues), or come say
hi in [Discussions](https://github.com/warpedatom/warpedatom.github.io/discussions).

More soon.
